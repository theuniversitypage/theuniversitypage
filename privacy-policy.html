Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026 • Effective Date: Immediately

At Abhinandan Updates, accessible from our educational portal, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information collected and recorded by us and how we use it.

1. Information We Collect

When you browse our portal or interact with our features, we may collect information directly or automatically:

  • Personal Information: Name, email address, or contact details provided voluntarily via our Contact Us form.
  • Usage Data: Pages visited, time spent on updates, referral URLs, browser type, and device information.

2. How We Use Your Information

The information we collect is used in the following ways:

  • To provide, operate, and maintain our educational updates portal.
  • To improve, personalize, and expand user experience.
  • To understand and analyze how visitors interact with our content.
  • To send periodic updates, newsletters, or respond to user queries.
  • To prevent fraudulent activities and ensure platform security.

3. Log Files

Abhinandan Updates follows a standard procedure of using log files. These files log visitors when they visit websites. The information collected includes IP addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date/time stamp, referring/exit pages, and number of clicks. This data is not linked to any personally identifiable information.

4. Cookies and Web Beacons

Like any other website, Abhinandan Updates uses 'cookies'. These cookies are used to store information including visitors' preferences and the pages on the website that the visitor accessed. The information is used to optimize the users' experience by customizing our web page content based on visitors' browser type or other information.

5. Google DoubleClick DART Cookie

Google is one of the third-party vendors on our site. It also uses cookies, known as DART cookies, to serve ads to our site visitors based upon their visit to our site and other sites on the internet. Visitors may choose to decline the use of DART cookies by visiting the Google ad and content network Privacy Policy.

6. Third Party Privacy Policies

Abhinandan Updates's Privacy Policy does not apply to other advertisers or websites. Thus, we are advising you to consult the respective Privacy Policies of these third-party ad servers for more detailed information.

7. Contact Us

If you have additional questions or require more information about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to reach out via our Contact Us page.
