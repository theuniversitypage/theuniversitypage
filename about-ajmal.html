Jeevan Parichay & Journey

Ajmal Mansoori ek energetic student leader, mobile application developer, aur educational innovator hain. Unhone university-level entrance exams aur counseling processes ki jatilta (complexity) ko dekhte hue digital education sector me apna kadam rakha.

Unhone apne colleague Manjeet Sir ke sath milkar Learnify Classes aur CUET Learnify ki neev rakhi. Unka uddeshya Prayagraj (Allahabad University), BHU, DU, aur central universities me admission lene wale chhatro ko authentic aur accurate updates bina kisi late fee ya confusion ke pradan karna hai.
