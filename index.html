Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers regarding admissions, cutoffs, credit system, and counseling

What is the Allahabad University NEP 2026 Credit System?

Under NEP 2020/2026, students earn 44 credits/year (184 total credits across 4 years) to receive an Honours with Research degree. Watch Ajmal Mansoori's detailed video guide above for semester-wise breakdown.

How do I apply for BHU CUET UG Admission 2026?

After CUET results are announced, BHU launches its CSAS portal. Candidates select domain combinations (General Test + Language for BA, PCM/PCB for BSc) to get shortlisted for FMC and MMV seats.

How accurate is the College Predictor tool?

Our predictor processes past seat cutoffs and rank trends for Central Universities (AU, BHU, DU) to give an accurate estimation of your admission chances.

Where can I access free mock tests and e-books?

You can access free mock tests and booklets directly via the Learnify Classes portal link in the Accelerator Tools section at the top of the page.

How do I track UP Scholarship form updates?

Click 'Scholarships' in the Quick Link slider or sidebar drawer to get direct links for Dashmottar renewal, status check, and fresh registrations.
