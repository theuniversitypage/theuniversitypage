Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Allahabad University NEP Credit System?

Under NEP, students complete 44 credits annually (184 credits across 4 years) to receive an Honours with Research degree. Check the Syllabus section for complete semester-wise course combinations.

How accurate is the Campus Rank Predictor tool?

The predictor models previous years' merit cutoff drops, seat matrix allocations, and reservation statistics for Central Universities (AU, BHU, DU) to provide a realistic rank estimation.
