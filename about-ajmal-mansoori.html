    An all-in-one entrance preparation platform offering premium mock test series, digital e-books, and structured test modules for CUET UG, CUET PG, PGAT, BHU Entrance, and Central Universities.
  • The University Page (Founded 2026)

Vision & Objective

Core Mission: To eliminate the guidance gap for students aspiring for Central and State Universities. Having experienced the challenges of navigating complex entrance exams and admission portals, Ajmal Mansoori established The University Page and Learnify Classes to provide unhindered access to verified cutoffs, syllabus resources, and mock tests in one unified ecosystem.
